Academics & Learning

A structured academic environment where high academic expectations are matched with supportive, personalised instruction and comprehensive extracurricular development.

Our Academic Philosophy

Developing Independent, Critical Thinkers

At Achievers Academy, academic life is organised around the belief that true learning involves deep comprehension rather than passive memorisation.

We combine structured curriculum frameworks with inquiry-driven teaching methods. Our faculty encourages students to question assumptions, synthesize concepts across disciplines, and demonstrate their understanding through written analysis, verbal debate, and collaborative projects.

Every stage of learning is designed to prepare students smoothly for subsequent educational milestones, university preparation, and real-world leadership.

Progression Pathways

Educational Stages & Classes

Organised stages that provide age-appropriate academic challenge, pastoral care, and skill development at every milestone.

Foundation Stage

Early Years & Kindergarten

Nursery & Reception (Ages 3 – 5)

Cultivating curiosity, language acquisition, social confidence, and early numeracy through structured, purposeful play and guided sensory discovery.

Core Learning Focus:

  • Phonics, early reading & expressive communication
  • Number concepts, sorting & spatial patterns
  • Gross and fine motor skills development
  • Social sharing, emotional empathy & habits

Primary Stage

Primary School

Grades 1 – 6 (Ages 6 – 11)

Establishing rock-solid fundamentals in core literacy, mathematical reasoning, scientific enquiry, social studies, and creative expression.

Core Learning Focus:

  • Advanced literacy, grammar & creative writing
  • Mathematical reasoning & applied mental arithmetic
  • Basic science, experimental curiosity & nature study
  • Digital literacy, typing & introductory computer science

Secondary Stage

Junior Secondary School

JSS 1 – 3 / Grades 7 – 9 (Ages 11 – 14)

Transitioning learners from generalist instruction to specialised subject areas, developing systematic study skills, and fostering independent inquiry.

Core Learning Focus:

  • Integrated sciences: Physics, Chemistry & Biology
  • Algebraic mathematics, geometry & data analysis
  • Literature in English, history, geography & civics
  • Information & Communication Technology (ICT)

Senior Secondary School

SSS 1 – 3 / Grades 10 – 12 (Ages 14 – 17)

Classroom Excellence

Our Teaching & Assessment Methodology

How we ensure that curriculum plans translate into meaningful, measurable student progress.

Qualified Subject Specialists

Teachers with deep academic domain expertise who inspire interest through clarity of instruction, interactive discussions, and real-world examples.

Continuous Formative Assessment

Weekly progress checks, classroom participation assessments, and homework diagnostics identify areas where learners need reinforcement before term-end examinations.

Individualized Remediation

Targeted academic support and catch-up sessions for students who require additional clarification in mathematics, sciences, or language skills.

Academic Calendar Structure

A balanced three-term academic year with dedicated weeks for curriculum delivery, mid-term evaluations, revision clinics, and final examinations.

Beyond the Classroom

Student Development & Extracurriculars

Education extends far beyond textbooks. Our co-curricular programmes foster collaboration, self-discipline, and lifelong physical and creative passions.

STEM & Robotics Club

Hands-on coding challenges, microcontroller projects, science fairs, and logical problem-solving that bring theory to life.

Debating & Public Speaking

Cultivating eloquent articulation, structured argumentation, active listening, and parliamentary debating skills.

Creative Arts & Drama

Fine arts, painting, ceramics, theatrical performance, and annual school productions that inspire imaginative confidence.

Music & Instrumental Choir

Vocal harmony training, keyboard, string and percussion tuition, and participation in school assemblies and musical showcases.

Athletics & Team Sports

Football, track and field, basketball, and table tennis. Promoting cardiovascular endurance, fair play, and athletic discipline.

Community Service Society

Environmental awareness drives, charity food banks, literacy outreach, and neighbourhood clean-up initiatives.
