About Achievers Academy

Dedicated to academic distinction, strong personal discipline, and fostering a collaborative learning community where every student is challenged to achieve their potential.

Our School Community

An Environment Designed for Purposeful Growth

Achievers Academy is an independent educational institution established to provide rigorous intellectual training alongside principled moral education.

Located in Calabar, Cross River State, our academy provides a welcoming and focused atmosphere where students from diverse backgrounds discover their talents, develop intellectual curiosity, and cultivate the habits of mind essential for modern society.

We view education as an enduring partnership between home, school, and community. Our teachers are mentors who hold high expectations for every student while offering the support, guidance, and encouragement needed to achieve them.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

The principles that guide our conduct, inspire our standards, and shape every interaction within our school community.

Integrity

Doing what is right, honest, and ethical, even when unobserved. We place personal honesty at the centre of scholarship.

Excellence

Striving constantly for one's highest personal standard in academic studies, sportsmanship, and creative pursuits.

Curiosity

Embracing learning as a lifelong journey. We encourage students to ask probing questions, investigate facts, and think independently.

Resilience

Understanding that setbacks are opportunities for reflection and growth. We teach perseverance and constructive self-management.

Respect

Valuing each member of our community, honouring diverse perspectives, and practicing courtesy and empathy every day.

Service

Recognising our responsibility to help others and actively contribute towards the betterment of our wider neighbourhood and nation.

Our Pedagogical Framework

How We Nurture Every Student

At Achievers Academy, academic performance is inseparable from emotional wellbeing and social confidence. Our approach is built on three central pillars:

  • Individual Mentorship: Small class sizes ensure that educators know each child's academic strengths, areas for development, and personal learning preferences.
  • Active & Practical Learning: We combine direct classroom instruction with hands-on laboratory experiments, library research, and oral presentations.
  • Parent-School Collaboration: Regular academic progress updates, structured parent consultations, and open communication channels.
