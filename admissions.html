Admissions & Enrollment

We welcome ambitious, curious, and motivated young learners into our school community. Learn about our admissions steps, review document requirements, and submit your application online.

Join Our School

Welcoming Prospective Families

Deciding on the right school for your child is one of the most important investments in their future. At Achievers Academy, we strive to make the admissions journey straightforward, transparent, and supportive.

We admit students based on their developmental readiness, academic potential, and compatibility with our school values of honesty, hard work, and mutual respect.

Our admissions team is available throughout the week to answer questions, guide parents through documentation, and schedule campus appointments.

Important Admissions Policy Note:

Submission of an application form initiates the evaluation process but does not automatically guarantee admission. Final offers are made following entrance assessments and parent-student interviews subject to class capacity.

How to Apply

The 4-Step Application Journey

A clear and structured pathway from initial application to official student enrollment.

1

Complete Application

Fill out the online application form below with accurate student details and parent or guardian contact information.

2

Document Review

Submit copies of birth certificate, previous school academic transcripts, and recent passport-sized photographs to admissions.

3

Assessment & Visit

The student completes an age-appropriate entrance assessment, followed by a friendly interaction with our academic leadership.

4

Offer & Enrollment

Successful candidates receive an official Letter of Admission with registration guidelines and orientation schedule.

Standard Document Requirements

Admissions Checklist

Please ensure you have digital or physical copies of the following documents ready when invited for the assessment stage:

Copy of Student's Official Birth Certificate
Previous Two Terms' Academic Reports / Transcripts
Two Recent Passport-Sized Photographs
Student Immunisation Record or Medical Certificate
Valid Identification of Parent or Legal Guardian
Transfer Certificate (for students joining intermediate classes)

* Note: Do not attach physical sensitive documents or make payments online in this first stage. Documents will be inspected during in-person verification.
